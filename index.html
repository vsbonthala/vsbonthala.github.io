About

I am a computational biologist with over ten years of research experience in plant genomics. I build reproducible pipelines that turn long-read sequencing data into chromosome-scale, haplotype-resolved genomes, annotations and pangenomes, and I work closely with breeders and experimental biologists to turn genomic data into answers they can use.

At the Julius Kühn-Institut I lead the haplotype-resolved assembly of 120 autotetraploid potato genomes. Before that I led comparative genome assembly of 37 Asteraceae species at Heinrich-Heine-Universität Düsseldorf, and worked on the barley pan-genome at Helmholtz Munich.
